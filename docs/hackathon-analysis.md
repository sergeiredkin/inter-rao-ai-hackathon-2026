# Hackathon Analysis

## Strategic Assessment

The hackathon is valuable primarily as a career and portfolio opportunity rather than only as a competition for the prize fund.

The strongest alignment is with an Energy AI positioning:

- Industrial domain expertise
- LLM engineering
- Enterprise automation
- Process optimization

## Track Evaluation

### 1. AI presentation assistant

Pros:
- Easy to prototype
- Clear demo

Cons:
- High competition
- Limited differentiation

Assessment: low strategic value.

### 2. Court document analysis assistant

Pros:
- Interesting NLP pipeline
- Document understanding use case

Cons:
- Less aligned with energy domain expertise.

Assessment: technically interesting but weaker positioning.

### 3. IT/IB digest assistant

Pros:
- Fits OSINT and security automation experience.
- Possible enterprise value.

Recommended evolution:

Not only summarization, but intelligence analysis:

- Source collection
- Entity extraction
- Impact assessment
- Risk prioritization
- Recommended actions

### 4. BPMN Architect

Recommended primary direction.

Evolution from simple diagram generation:

AI Process Engineer for Energy

Capabilities:

- Understand operational procedures
- Generate BPMN 2.0 workflows
- Validate process logic
- Identify bottlenecks
- Suggest automation opportunities

## Positioning

Avoid positioning as a generic chatbot.

Position as:

> AI assistant for industrial process engineering and digital transformation.

## Important Constraints

Keep hackathon implementation isolated from existing personal projects.

Use a dedicated repository and avoid transferring proprietary architectures or reusable commercial components.
